Ambient Video

Ask for a long-form idea. If you approve it, the app builds a nearly still picture, a seamless sound loop, and a video you can send to YouTube.

The picture is one still with a very slow zoom. The rain, noise, or fire is the sound, not animation.

Shorts

Ten vertical 30-second videos. Each row updates as that Short is built and uploaded. This batch runs on its own, alongside a long video.
