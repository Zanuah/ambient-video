# Sound bites

Rain, waves, a stream, wind, and a fire are uncompressed 48 kHz recordings, about 13 seconds each, looped for the whole video. Brown, pink, and white noise are still synthesized in `app/audio.py`.

These replaced earlier clips that were 16 kHz or compressed, which left a hiss under the rain and waves.

| Profile | Clip | Recording | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Soft rain | `soft_rain.wav` | Steady rain on leaves and ground, with the harsh top rolled off | CC0 | https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects/ambient |
| Heavy rain | `heavy_rain.wav` | The same rain recording, kept brighter | CC0 | https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects/ambient |
| Ocean waves | `ocean.wav` | Waves washing onto a sandy beach | CC0 | https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects/ambient |
| Stream | `stream.wav` | A stream flowing over rocks | CC0 | https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects/ambient |
| Wind | `wind.wav` | Wind through trees | CC0 | https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects/ambient |
| Fireplace | `fireplace.wav` | Crackling campfire | CC0 | https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects/ambient |

Each file is 48 kHz, mono, 16-bit. The source page releases the recordings under CC0.
