# Sound bites

Rain, waves, stream, wind, and fire are 16-second clips cut from free recordings, crossfaded so the join does not click, then looped for the whole video. Brown, pink, and white noise are still synthesized in `app/audio.py`.

| Profile | Clip | Recording | Creator | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Soft rain | `soft_rain.wav` | Rain (loopable), track 3. Rain on a window. | Ylmir | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable |
| Heavy rain | `heavy_rain.wav` | Rain (loopable), track 2. Heavier rain from the same session. | Ylmir | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable |
| Ocean waves | `ocean.wav` | On a pebble beach | earthcalling | Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:On_a_pebble_beach.ogg |
| Stream | `stream.wav` | Vistula river, short recording | RandomMind | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/sea-and-river-wave-sounds |
| Wind | `wind.wav` | Howling wind | Tvabutzku1234 | CC0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Howling_wind.ogg |
| Fireplace | `fireplace.wav` | Fireplace sound loop | PagDev | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/fireplace-sound-loop |

Each clip is 16 seconds, 48 kHz, mono. The original files are longer. These copies are trimmed and crossfaded for looping.
